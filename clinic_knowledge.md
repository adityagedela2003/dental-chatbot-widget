## Treatments & Price List

### 1. Routine Dental Checkup & Cleaning
- **Description:** Full oral examination, teeth cleaning (scaling), and polishing. Includes X-ray if needed.
- **Duration:** 45–60 minutes
- **Price:** ₹500 – ₹800
- **Recommended:** Every 6 months

### 2. Teeth Whitening (Bleaching)
- **Description:** Professional in-clinic bleaching to remove stains and lighten teeth by 3–8 shades.
- **Duration:** 60–90 minutes
- **Price:** ₹3,000 – ₹6,000
- **Note:** Results last 12–18 months with proper care. Not suitable for patients with crowns or veneers.

### 3. Dental Fillings (Tooth Coloured / Composite)
- **Description:** Removal of decay and filling with tooth-coloured composite resin. Virtually invisible.
- **Duration:** 30–60 minutes per tooth
- **Price:** ₹800 – ₹1,500 per tooth
- **Note:** Silver (amalgam) fillings also available at ₹500 per tooth.

### 4. Root Canal Treatment (RCT)
- **Description:** Removal of infected pulp from inside the tooth to save it from extraction. Followed by crown placement.
- **Duration:** 2–3 sittings of 45–60 minutes each
- **Price:** ₹3,500 – ₹7,000 per tooth (excluding crown)
- **Note:** Front teeth are on the lower end; molars on the higher end.

### 5. Dental Crown (Cap)
- **Description:** A custom-made cap placed over a damaged or root-canal-treated tooth.
- **Types & Price:**
  - Metal (PFM) Crown: ₹2,500 – ₹4,000
  - Zirconia (all-ceramic) Crown: ₹6,000 – ₹10,000
- **Duration:** 2 sittings over 5–7 days

### 6. Tooth Extraction (Simple)
- **Description:** Removal of a severely damaged, decayed, or mobile tooth under local anaesthesia.
- **Duration:** 20–40 minutes
- **Price:** ₹500 – ₹1,500 per tooth
- **Note:** Surgical extraction (impacted/wisdom teeth) costs ₹2,000 – ₹5,000.

### 7. Wisdom Tooth Removal (Surgical Extraction)
- **Description:** Removal of impacted or partially erupted wisdom teeth under local anaesthesia.
- **Duration:** 45–90 minutes
- **Price:** ₹2,000 – ₹5,000 per tooth
- **Note:** X-ray/OPG required before procedure (₹300 – ₹500 extra).

### 8. Dental Implants
- **Description:** A titanium screw surgically placed in the jawbone to replace a missing tooth root, topped with a crown.
- **Duration:** 3–6 months total (healing + crown placement)
- **Price:** ₹25,000 – ₹45,000 per implant (all inclusive)
- **Note:** One of the most permanent tooth replacement solutions. Requires bone density assessment.

### 9. Dentures (Removable)
- **Description:** Custom-made removable prosthetics to replace multiple or all missing teeth.
- **Types & Price:**
  - Partial Denture: ₹5,000 – ₹12,000
  - Full Denture (upper or lower): ₹10,000 – ₹20,000
- **Duration:** 3–5 sittings over 2–3 weeks

### 10. Dental Braces (Orthodontic Treatment)
- **Description:** Correction of misaligned teeth and bite issues using braces.
- **Types & Price:**
  - Metal Braces: ₹18,000 – ₹30,000
  - Ceramic Braces: ₹25,000 – ₹40,000
  - Invisible Aligners (like Invisalign): ₹60,000 – ₹1,20,000
- **Duration:** 12–24 months depending on severity
- **Note:** Includes all adjustment sittings. Retainers charged separately (₹2,000 – ₹5,000).

### 11. Veneers (Porcelain / Composite)
- **Description:** Thin shells bonded to the front of teeth to improve appearance — shape, colour, size.
- **Types & Price:**
  - Composite Veneers: ₹3,000 – ₹5,000 per tooth
  - Porcelain Veneers: ₹8,000 – ₹15,000 per tooth
- **Duration:** 1–2 sittings

### 12. Gum Treatment (Periodontal Therapy)
- **Description:** Treatment for gum disease including deep cleaning (scaling & root planing), curettage, and gum surgery if needed.
- **Price:**
  - Scaling & Root Planing: ₹1,500 – ₹3,000 (full mouth)
  - Gum Surgery (flap surgery): ₹5,000 – ₹12,000 per quadrant
- **Duration:** 1–3 sittings

### 13. Paediatric Dentistry (Children's Dental Care)
- **Description:** Dental care for children aged 3–16 including checkups, fluoride treatment, pit & fissure sealants, and child-friendly fillings.
- **Price:**
  - Checkup & Cleaning: ₹400 – ₹600
  - Fluoride Treatment: ₹500 – ₹800
  - Pit & Fissure Sealants: ₹600 – ₹1,000 per tooth
- **Note:** We have a friendly, child-safe environment with trained paediatric staff.

### 14. Teeth Sensitivity Treatment
- **Description:** Diagnosis and treatment of sensitive teeth using desensitising agents, fluoride varnish, or bonding.
- **Duration:** 30–45 minutes
- **Price:** ₹500 – ₹1,500 depending on severity
- **Note:** May require multiple sittings for chronic sensitivity.

### 15. Emergency Dental Care
- **Description:** Same-day or next-day appointments for toothaches, broken teeth, lost fillings, swelling, or dental trauma.
- **Availability:** Mon–Sat, call +91 98765 43210
- **Consultation Fee:** ₹300 – ₹500 (adjusted against treatment cost)
- **Note:** For life-threatening emergencies (severe swelling, difficulty breathing/swallowing), please go to the nearest ER immediately.

## Clinic Policies

### Appointment Policy
- All appointments must be booked in advance by calling the clinic or via the website chatbot.
- Walk-ins are accepted based on availability but appointments are prioritized.
- Please arrive 10 minutes before your scheduled appointment.

### Cancellation Policy
- Cancellations must be made at least 24 hours in advance.
- Late cancellations (less than 24 hours) may incur a ₹200 cancellation fee.
- No-shows will be charged ₹300.

### Payment Policy
- Accepted modes: Cash, UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), Debit/Credit Cards, Net Banking.
- EMI available for treatments above ₹10,000 (0% EMI for 3–6 months on select cards).
- Full payment or 50% advance required for implants and orthodontic treatments.

### Insurance Policy
- We accept cashless treatment for the following insurers: Star Health, HDFC ERGO, Niva Bupa, Care Health.
- Please carry your insurance card and pre-authorisation letter if applicable.
- Cosmetic procedures (whitening, veneers) are NOT covered by insurance.

### Hygiene & Safety Policy
- All instruments are sterilised using an autoclave after every patient.
- Disposable items (gloves, masks, syringes) are single-use only.
- Clinic is sanitised between every patient.
- We follow Indian Dental Association (IDA) infection control guidelines.

### New Patient Policy
- New patients are requested to arrive 15 minutes early to fill out a medical history form.
- Please inform us of any allergies, medications, or medical conditions (diabetes, heart disease, blood thinners) before treatment.

### Children Policy
- Children under 12 must be accompanied by a parent or guardian.
- We offer a friendly waiting area with toys and cartoons to keep children comfortable.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is the treatment painful?**
A: We use local anaesthesia for all invasive procedures. Most patients feel little to no pain during treatment. Post-procedure mild discomfort is normal and manageable with prescribed medication.

**Q: How long does a root canal take?**
A: Typically 2–3 sittings spread over 1–2 weeks. Each sitting is 45–60 minutes.

**Q: At what age should I bring my child for their first dental visit?**
A: Ideally by age 3, or as soon as their first teeth appear. Early visits help prevent cavities and build a positive relationship with dental care.

**Q: Do you offer home visits?**
A: Currently we do not offer home visits. All treatments are performed at our clinic.

**Q: Can I get braces as an adult?**
A: Absolutely! There is no age limit for orthodontic treatment. We have many adult patients on braces and aligners.

**Q: How do I manage a toothache before my appointment?**
A: Take an over-the-counter painkiller (like Ibuprofen or Paracetamol) as directed. Avoid very hot, cold, or sweet foods. Do not put aspirin directly on the tooth. Call us for an emergency slot if pain is severe.
